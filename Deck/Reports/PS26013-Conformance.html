Clause group 1 of 5 · four verbs

The platform definition

"An Al-enabled geospatial integration platform capable of automatically integrating, harmonizing, validating, and synchronizing multiple land-related datasets with Al-generated feature extraction outputs." Four verbs, and they are not synonyms.

ClauseStatusWhat answers it
Integratingmet Five configured layers ingested and put on one datum in a single run — 14,253 + 1,826 parcels, 55,810 + 70,776 footprints. ingest, crs.engine.
Harmonizingmet 106,077 entities assembled from 558,294 claims across sources; 36,987 conflicts adjudicated, 42.7% closed automatically. matching, conflict.
Validatingmet 15 topology rules with bounded, refusable repair; six-dimension confidence graded A–E on every feature; hash-chained ledger, 20 entries, chain verified. topology, confidence, core.ledger.
Synchronizingpartial POST /api/subscriptions and the delivery log are implemented and the AOI / feature-class / change-type / confidence filters exist. Webhook delivery has not been exercised against a second real department — until it is, this is a loop-back test, and we say so.
Why "validating" is the one to press us on

Most submissions read validate as "check the geometry is valid". Here it means the platform can be audited: every resolution names the claims it superseded, carries a plain-language rationale, and is anchored in a Merkle root a citizen can check without trusting the department. Run geovax verify --out out/chennai_metro and it either reports chain intact over 20 entries or names the exact index where it breaks.

The two clauses that decide whether this is a land-records platform

Conflict resolution and confidence are last in the statement and first in the design. When four sources disagree about one boundary, something must decide and something must say how sure it is. The platform never averages. It selects a boundary some surveyor actually observed, by evidence — because an averaged boundary is attributable to no survey, can be asked of no surveyor, and is indefensible in a dispute.

What the final solution is judged on

Stated outcomeStatusEvidence
Reduce manual GIS integration effortmet 106,077 features reconciled in 241.6 s on 2 vCPU, unattended, with per-stage checkpoints. The manual equivalent is a GIS analyst reconciling parcel by parcel.
Improve accuracy and consistencymet Two undeclared systematic offsets recovered and removed (1.51 m @ 073°, 3.41 m @ 309°); topological consistency raised to 0.9997; 860 apparent mutations correctly suppressed as re-registration rather than raised.

Accelerate cadastral finalizationmet Finalisation is blocked by contested parcels. 42.7% of 36,987 conflicts close without a human; the remaining 21,192 are ranked by decision value and batched by cause into 2 groups, so one decision generalises across hundreds of cases.
Improve interoperabilitymet Open standards on every outbound path: OGC API - Features (ISO 19168-1), GeoJSON RFC 7946, CityJSON 1.1 for the LOD1 city model, Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF for raster, LGD codes as the join key.

Scope

What this record supports, stated as it should be said

This record supports one sentence: on 22.3 km² of central Chennai, using five real government layers and no synthetic data, the platform automatically integrated, harmonised and validated 106,077 land features in 241.6 seconds on 2 vCPU, closed 42.7% of the conflicts without a human, and published a per-feature confidence grade and an auditable reason for every decision it made.

It does not support a claim about accuracy against ground truth, because there is no ground truth here to measure against. It does not support a claim about a second department receiving a webhook, because none has. And it does not support any claim about title.

The six partial rows are the honest half of this document

Ground truthing and GNSS/CORS are partial because the real data requires credentials this environment cannot obtain. Synchronisation and inter-departmental exchange are partial because the far end of a webhook has to be a real department. Spatial databases and cloud are partial by choice, and the choices are defended above. A conformance record with no partial rows is a marketing document.
